# Wellcall System Limitations & Safety Disclaimers

> [!CAUTION]
> **SAFETY & MEDICAL DISCLAIMER**
> **Wellcall** is an experimental hackathon prototype created for the STARFORGE 2026 VoxForge track. It is **NOT** a certified medical device and must **NOT** be used for real clinical diagnosis, autonomous medical treatment, or unmonitored triage.

---

## Technical Limitations & Boundaries

1. **Synthetic Data Policy**:
   - All patient data, care plans, and red-flag symptoms are 100% synthetic. No real PHI is stored or processed.

2. **Triage Assistance vs Diagnosis**:
   - Wellcall assists nurses by screening post-discharge outreach responses. It does not provide medical diagnoses.

3. **False-Negative Risk Safeguards**:
   - NLP extractions and semantic vector matching may misinterpret non-standard phrasing or dialects. The system defaults to conservative escalation for ambiguous symptoms.

4. **Clinical Validation Requirement**:
   - Formal clinical trials, IRB approval, and HIPAA compliance validation are required prior to any real-world healthcare deployment.
